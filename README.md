 3. Protection Against Solar Inverter Feedback Noise
* The Current Issue: 
    There is a rapid boom of rooftop solar installations across the Philippines. Cheap, poorly filtered on-grid solar inverters can inject heavy harmonic noise and DC injection backward into the local utility lines.
* The FluxGuard-Pro15k Impact: 
   Acting as a bi-directional passive filter at the main breaker, the device locks down DC bias and electrical noise. 
   It would stop your neighbor’s solar setup from distorting your electricity, and stop your appliances from polluting the grid line back to the substation.
