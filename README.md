Note to Users: 
   Thanks to FluxGuard-Pro15k industrial-grade core, heavy household heating appliances and digital electronics can be safely plugged into this distribution unit simultaneously. However, because digital screens and heating coils do not use magnetic transformers, they do not experience core saturation and will pass through cleanly without additional efficiency adjustments
