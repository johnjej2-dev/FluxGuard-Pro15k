### Safety Standards & Professional Requirements
Connecting a device like the FluxGuard-Pro15k directly to a main service panel involves working with high-voltage electrical systems. For safety and compliance, the following standards apply:

* Licensed Professional Mandatory: 
    Installation at the main switch or circuit breaker level must be performed exclusively by a licensed electrician or registered electrical engineer. Main panels contain uninsulated feeder lines that carry lethal voltage, posing a severe risk of fatal electric shock or arc flash. [1, 2] 

* Code Compliance: 
   All connections must adhere to national and local electrical codes (such as the NEC or equivalent). Improper wiring at the panel level can create fire hazards, void insurance policies, and violate building regulations.

* System De-energization: 
   Working on a main distribution board requires the complete disconnection of the primary utility service. Verification of a zero-energy state using calibrated, professional-grade testing equipment is a critical safety requirement before opening any enclosure
