### Installation Guide
⚠️ DANGER: RISK OF ELECTRIC SHOCK. Installation must be performed exclusively by a licensed electrician or certified HVAC technician. Ensure the main circuit breaker at your home’s distribution panel is turned OFF before attempting installation.
## Wiring Diagram & Connections

[220V-240V MAIN PANEL] ───> [20A Type C Breaker] ───> [FLUXGUARD IN Line]
                                                           │
                                                      (Internal Matrix)
                                                           │
[FACTORY APPLIANCE (2.5HP)] <─── [Safety Disconnect] <─── [FLUXGUARD OUT Line]

   1. Mounting: 
   Securely mount the FluxGuard Pro enclosure to a flat surface in a well-ventilated area away from direct moisture, rainfall, or extreme ambient heat sources.
   2. Input Feed: 
   Route the incoming AC Line (Hot) from a dedicated 20-Amp Type C Circuit Breaker into the terminal marked LINE INPUT (IN).
   3. Output Feed: 
   Connect the wire leading directly to the appliance or local safety disconnect switch into the terminal marked LOAD OUTPUT (OUT).
   4. Neutral & Ground: 
   Pass the Neutral line and System Ground wire directly through the enclosure's accessory routing tracks to the appliance. The FluxGuard is an inline device that intercepts and conditions the Hot Line only.
   5. Torque Verification: 
   Ensure all heavy-duty terminal block screws are torqued tightly to prevent high contact resistance and localized heating.
